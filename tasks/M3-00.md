## Preregistered experiment

The baseline preserves the accepted menu path and riding inputs through frame
2999, then continues holding Right until a bounded maximum of 12,000 PAL frames.
The first exploratory run may stop earlier only after frame images and observed
state prove that the race has finished and a stable result/post-race state has
been reached. No finish address, frame, outcome, or required duration is assumed.

Before either replay is run, freeze a controller variation with Right released
for frames 3000–3299 and held again from frame 3300 to the same maximum. The
preregistered predictions are: both scenarios agree through frame 2999; the
first difference is the controller image at frame 3000; player speed/progress
subsequently differs; if both complete the race, the variation does not finish
earlier than the continuously accelerated baseline. A failure to finish within
the bound or an opponent-first result is an observation, not grounds to rewrite
these predictions after the run.
