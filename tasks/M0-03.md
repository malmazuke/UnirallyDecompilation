## Outcome and boundaries

Compare integration effort for a pinned bsnes core and Mesen Community Edition for headless programmatic execution of the identified PAL ROM. Implement, for the selected candidate: load and identify the ROM bytes; deterministic initial SRAM and reset; bounded input delivery at a defined point; capture of memory, relevant registers and a trace window without a GUI; save/restore. Repeat a cold-start run three times in fresh processes with identical sampled outputs. Record the decision in `docs/decisions/D-0001-reference-emulator.md`.

Not in scope: any gameplay interpretation, comparator, replay manifest schema (M0-04), or building the emulator into the release.
