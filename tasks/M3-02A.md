## Outcome and boundaries

Implement the D-0005 boundary for the accepted PAL ROM: one supported ROM is
verified and converted locally into a deterministic, versioned Classic content
pack whose entries are addressed by logical IDs. The native runtime can load
the validated pack without a ROM or loose extraction tree being present.

Keep the first schema limited to content required by the M3 Crawler/DRAGSTER
slice, while allowing M3-02 to add presentation entries without exposing ROM
offsets to the renderer. Preserve the existing directory-backed loader where it
is useful for research, but give it and the pack reader the same logical content
contract.

Not in scope: extracting every game asset, adding another ROM revision, public
release, legal clearance, replacement-art creation, a general mod marketplace,
SDL UI, renderer implementation, audio, or changing M3-01 gameplay behavior.
