## Outcome and boundaries

Deliver a reproducible identity for the user-selected PAL Unirally ROM and the first stable CLI entry point:

1. `python3 tools/project.py rom inspect --path <file>` hashes the original bytes without modifying them, identifies the SNES header location, mapping, region, internal checksum validity and interrupt vectors, and writes a manifest.
2. `--expect <manifest>` compares an input against the tracked manifest and fails clearly on an unexpected revision.
3. A tracked manifest in `tests/manifests/rom/` records the identity. The ROM bytes stay in ignored local storage.
4. A research record documents the evidence and its limits.

Not in scope: disassembly, extraction, any emulator work, normalizing or de-headering ROM images. The header decode is diagnostic; region and mapping are read from the header and are not yet confirmed by execution.
