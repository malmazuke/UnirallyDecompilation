On this ROM two fresh runs of `boot-start-600` are identical (sample digest `583d1ec544ec61a2…`); removing or moving the Start press at frames 300–305 is reported as a first divergence at frame 300 and localized to one work RAM byte, `$7E0073` (`0x10` held, `0x00` not), with identical final states; absent ROM/state/evidence exit 2, an unreachable manifest exits 4, invalid manifests exit 3. Two independent reviews with their own withheld perturbations agreed with manual frame-by-frame comparisons. A state is a valid origin only with its restore-check report at the same save point.

- Observed code map (M1-01, [R-0006](research/R-0006-observed-code-map.md)): whole-run instruction coverage is captured by draining the pinned core's unchanged trace ring once per frame from Python (`python3 tools/project.py coverage capture|map`; the core patch, lock and every existing manifest and digest are unchanged, and the boot scenario's sample digest is identical under capture). Independently reproduced by one reviewer from its own clone, including two own perturbations and frame images. Suite 118 → 148 checks. Observed effort: about 30 min of worker session plus one 10.5 min review (the worker's handoff overstated its own duration; the harness measurement is recorded in the task).
- Trace-derived access record and player state (M1-02, [D-0002](decisions/D-0002-data-access-observation.md), [R-0007](research/R-0007-player-state.md), schema [docs/state/player-state.md](state/player-state.md)): reads, writes and stored values are derived from the unchanged trace ring's per-instruction registers plus the ROM bytes (`python3 tools/project.py access capture|query`; the core, patch, locks, existing manifests and digests are unchanged). On the whole race scenario 25.9 M accesses derive in about 90 s with 0.24 % unresolved (pointers advanced by `INC` within the frame; no unresolved store); two fresh captures are byte-identical. Eleven fields of the race are recorded with their writer pcs and validated by nine new input-variant manifests whose predictions were written before each run: `pos_x` `$7E0415` (opponent `$7E0417`) with `pos[f+1] − pos[f]` equal to the displacement `$7E0BBB` stored in frame f+1 in every checked frame, `speed` `$7E04BB` decaying by exactly 1 per frame after a release, `throttle` `$7E0BEB` ±16 per frame, the race timer digits `$7E0E19`–`$7E0E29` at five PAL frames per tenth matching the on-screen tenths (one game update = one PAL frame from frame 1329; the sample at the end of frame N reflects frame N's inputs, in the order joypad → axes → physics → opponent → camera → timer), the joypad images `$7E0311`/`$7E0313` written right after the `$4218`/`$4219` reads. Lean (Up vs Down leaves no persistent work RAM state), the Y button, and the camera lag rule are recorded as unknown or identified only. Independently reproduced by one reviewer from its own clone with a withheld release at frame 2500; 9 of 10 mutations caught. Suite 148 → 173 checks. Observed effort: about 45 min plus 8 min of worker session (one usage-limit interruption with nothing lost), one 15.5 min review.
- Track and asset decode (M1-03, [R-0008](research/R-0008-track-decode.md), content record [docs/content/dragster-segment.md](content/dragster-segment.md), manifest `tests/manifests/content/dragster-segment.json`): with the PPU ports watched, the access record pairs all 39,705 DMA transfers of the race scenario with their destinations and decodes all 2,708 block moves (menus only). The race content is not moved by DMA or block move during the NMI-off load: a CPU port copier writes the font, BG2 tiles, tile map and palette raw from named ROM offsets, and the DRAGSTER track data (ROM file offset `0x0C0000`, 387 bytes) is an RNC ProPack method-1 stream unpacked by `$81:B8E2` into `$7F:0000`. A register-level port of that routine (`python3 tools/project.py content provenance|decode|compare`; `tools/unirally_lab/content/`) reproduces the original's 33,815-byte buffer except the loader's cursor byte, the per-tile tables 640/640 and flags 20/20; the 20 BG1 tiles are raw 64-byte DMAs from banks $16/$17 chosen by the tile-set list at the end of the block, the rider's tiles are streamed each frame from banks $27–$39, and the BG1 tile map is uploaded one 32-byte column per frame from a work RAM staging buffer. Rendering the decoded items with a small BG/sprite renderer matches the frame image except declared omissions (BG3 text, a four-line colour-math band under the ribbon, a window edge): 1.84 % of pixels at frame 1600, 1.58 % at 2400, 1.73 % at the reviewer's frame 2000, with the wall, ribbon and both riders pixel-identical. The physics reads the decoded per-tile table (`$81:8CBB`, values 0 and 0xA0 in this scenario) and ROM tables in banks $20/$21 that stay undecoded. Independently reproduced by one reviewer from its own clone with two withheld cases; 17 of 20 mutations caught. Suite 173 → 191 checks.
