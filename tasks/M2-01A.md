## Why this prerequisite exists

M2-01's frozen riding sequence is not causally independent of the opponent. At frame 1632 the player's speed cap changes because it reads opponent progress (R-0010, independently reproduced). Native sampling and marker progress have been recovered, but their component probes still receive original positions and poses. Those inputs cannot be replayed into an autonomous simulation.

The original post-gather response starts at `$81:8F98` and includes changes to position, velocity and persistent contact-related state. The opponent also jumps during the primary sequence: the recorded investigation locates an AI flag writer at `$83:E122`, postprocessing at `$83:E21F` and a jump trigger at `$82:A914`. These observations do not by themselves explain the mechanism. M1 did not validate jumps or the relevant pose/contact rules, and M2-01 excludes unvalidated trick/jump reconstruction. This work closes that prerequisite explicitly instead of substituting an always-accelerating opponent or relaxing required player fields.
