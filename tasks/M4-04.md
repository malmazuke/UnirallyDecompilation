## Outcome and boundaries

candidate continuation boundary for the accepted 3,300-frame ZOOM ZOO primary
and release-2500--2599 paths. Use end-of-frame 1649 as the proposed initial
observation and cover updates 1650--3299, preserving the opponent's accumulated
state. Determine the first observed native-domain blocker from original
evidence; this research task can be accepted without recovering every newly
encountered mechanic.

Do not implement native ZOOM ZOO, revise canonical serialization, alter the
existing DRAGSTER freezer/seed, expand the Classic pack, recover rendering/
camera/audio/result behavior, or generalize contact, AI or track formats.

## Why this prerequisite is required

M4-03 proves immutable content, two gathers and four collision calls, but stops
before contact response, pose recurrence, opponent AI and finish. The current
native path still supplies width 1024 where ZOOM ZOO observes 256, rejects
several non-flat/directional/horizontal contact branches, fixes opponent input
to 2 and uses DRAGSTER's finish threshold. Existing freeze/prepare tooling also
assumes the DRAGSTER frame-1533 seed and transient state. Changing hashes or
adding `0xC3` to the pack would conceal those dependencies.
