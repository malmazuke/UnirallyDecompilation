## Outcome and boundaries

Turn M4-02's identity-bound but opaque 50,665-byte ZOOM ZOO `0xC3` decode into
the smallest evidence-backed content/physics contract needed for later native
work. Locate and validate decoded layout regions, tile-set selection and rolling
BG1 gather against original runtime reads, and classify the bounded collision/
geometry inputs exercised by the frozen path.

Do not implement native ZOOM ZOO, expand the Classic pack, complete the race,
generalize a format from two tracks, recover presentation exhaustively or change
accepted DRAGSTER expectations. Unknown words remain opaque and decoded/original
bytes stay ignored.

recommendations plus coordinator decisions. Then reproduce the accepted M4-02
