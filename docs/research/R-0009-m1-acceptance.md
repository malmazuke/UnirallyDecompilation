- **The tile-map gather.** `$81:B31C` reads the decoded block at `$7F:000F,X` with `$0234`/`$0236`/`$0559`; the gather was read but not reproduced, and the BG1 map in every rendering comparison is the original's own upload sequence replayed from the work RAM series (R-0008 limits).
