## Outcome and boundaries

Demonstrate that the process, not the conversation, carries the work: a fresh session reproduces the M0-04 baseline from `docs/STATE.md`, `AGENTS.md` and the M0-04 handoff alone, then completes a bounded follow-up that the M0-04 reviewers left as a note: a ROM-free test of `replay compare`'s post-run wiring (`fresh_processes`, the required/optional `av_identical` rule, optional localization runs, the divergence report and exit codes) with a stubbed worker, so those paths are regression-safe without the ROM. Exercise interrupted-run recovery (a `replay compare` killed while a worker runs, then re-run from the handoff: no partial artifact may be mistaken for a result) and failed-test reporting (a deliberately failing test in a scratch worktree must make `test --suite synthetic` exit 1 and name the failing record; never a pass). Submit for independent review, then verify the exact candidate. Record the process gaps found before two workers are allowed to run concurrently.

Not in scope: a scheduler (a coordinator and durable records suffice), gameplay work (M1), native code, changes to the reference or replay formats.
