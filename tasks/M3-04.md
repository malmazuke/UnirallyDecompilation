## Outcome and boundaries

Demonstrate that the reviewed M3 components form one playable native
CRAWLER/DRAGSTER slice from a clean checkout: a user-supplied supported ROM
creates the Classic pack, the SDL app starts without original CPU execution,
live controls reach the deterministic simulation at PAL cadence, the complete
track can be played through the stable result, and a later launch succeeds from
the validated pack after the ROM is absent.

This is an evidence and integration task, not permission to expand the slice.
Audio, unrecovered intermediate rider artwork, other tracks/riders/modes,
menus/progression, local multiplayer and public packaging remain explicit
omissions. Do not call the whole game complete. M3 acceptance covers only the
identified PAL ROM and frozen one-player CRAWLER/DRAGSTER domain.

## Acceptance

1. From a fresh isolated checkout containing no generated content or prior
   build, bootstrap and build the app using tracked commands. With an ignored
   disposable supported ROM, `frontend run` exact-gates the ROM and atomically
   creates/validates the 25-entry pack; wrong ROM and interrupted/corrupt pack
   paths cannot pass.
2. Move the ROM copy and locator out of reach. The same checkout launches from
   the validated pack alone; record pack identity and prove no ROM open/fallback.
3. In a visible real SDL window, use actual keyboard events (and a gamepad if
   available) rather than `--fixed-controller-mask` to demonstrate press,
   simultaneous input where practical, release and focus-loss clearing. Record
   observable gameplay response without treating the hidden deterministic seam
   as live-input evidence.
4. Sustain the visible app for a complete continuous-right race through stable
   result or an equivalently bounded real-play sequence plus an exact full-race
   native run. Confirm the scene remains readable/current during held-art pose
   fallback and record the declared visual/audio omissions honestly.
5. Re-run the three accepted full-race gameplay inputs through stable result,
   restore boundaries spanning race/finish/result, all seven presentation cases
   at unchanged limits, debug/sanitizer suites and repository hygiene. No
   original CPU executes on the native side.
6. `R-0017` maps every M3 project-plan/build-validation gate to exact source,
   tool/input identities, commands and outcomes. Missing/skipped evidence blocks
   acceptance. A fresh reviewer reproduces clean-launch and owns at least one
   live-input or sustained-presentation boundary; hosted macOS/Linux CI passes
   the exact integration candidate.
7. Only after all gates pass, the coordinator updates state/registry, accepts
   M3-04 and milestone M3, creates annotated tag `m3`, and pushes the private
   repository. This does not authorize a public release or deployment.
