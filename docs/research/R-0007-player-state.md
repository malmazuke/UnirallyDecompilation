## Observation

### Part A — the access record

1. **Derivation.** Every ring entry (pc, A, X, Y, S, D, DBR, P, E before the instruction) is decoded with the opcode and operand bytes at its LoROM offset and the length under its M/X flags; `access/modes.py` gives the effective address, kind (read, write, read-modify-write, push, pull, block read, block write), width (M/X for data, 1–3 bytes for stack forms) and the stored value from the register the instruction names, for all 256 opcodes (ROM-free tests against hand-derived addresses for every addressing form, including the emulation-mode direct-page and stack page wraps, the 16-bit D wrap in bank 0, bank crossing of absolute and long indexed forms, push/pull directions and block moves). Loaded values are taken from the next entry's register. The indirect forms `(dp)`, `(dp,X)`, `(dp),Y`, `[dp]`, `[dp],Y`, `(sr),Y` and the operand bytes of code in work RAM are resolved per frame from the recorded stores of the same frame (`recorded_store`), the work RAM at the start of the frame (`start_of_frame`) or at its end (`end_of_frame`), and left unresolved when the last recorded write to a byte before the access has an unknown value; the labels are kept per (pc, addressing) and per access key.
2. **Cost and non-perturbation.** The whole race (3000 frames, 49,465,866 instructions) derives in 88 s of worker time (23 s for the 601-frame window 1500–2100); the sample digest and final state are the manifest's under every capture. The access total equals the samples' trace total and the sum of the per-frame deltas (whole run: 49,465,866 = the M1-01 coverage total).
3. **Extent (whole run).** 25,914,832 accesses; 255,898 distinct (pc, mode, addressing, kind, width, address) keys in work RAM and registers; 268 ROM-reading sites reading 189,993 distinct ROM bytes in 690 ranges; 39,705 DMA triggers and 1,754 HDMA enables logged with their channel parameters (28 DMA triggers in riding frame 1600); the two work RAM code sites `$00:0199` (88,769 executions) and `$00:019C` (2,708) decode from the recorded stores and the frame images. Resolution labels: `recorded_store` 151,697, `end_of_frame` 158,341, `start_of_frame` 24,779, `unresolved` 61,477 (0.24 % of all accesses; 0 in 1,258 of the 3000 frames; no unresolved *store*). The eight largest unresolved sites (`$82:80D1`, `$83:F27E`, `$83:F286`, `$82:82F0`, `$83:F24D`, `$83:F23B`, `$82:8141`, `$81:B9C4`; all `[dp]` or `[dp],Y`) account for 61,389 of them: their pointers at `$00:0076`/`$00:007C` are advanced by read-modify-write instructions (`INC dp` at `$81:B3E3`, `$83:F237`, `$83:F239`), so the value at the time of the access is unknown by construction. No resolution conflict occurred (0 bytes, 0 resolutions dropped).
4. **Repeatability.** Two fresh-process captures of the window 1500–2100 wrote byte-identical files (`464049b37dcdb267…`; an earlier pair differed only in the regeneration command that embedded the output path, corrected to a canonical form). The core `library_sha256` is kept out of the record (it varies per build, R-0002).
5. **Agreement with the real run.** The only writer of `$7E0313` over 1500–2100 is `$80:87F2` (STA abs, 8-bit, `0x01` in every frame with Right held) and it follows the reads of `$4218` at `$80:87E9` and `$4219` at `$80:87EF` (9 and 3 bytes earlier); `$7E0311` is written by `$80:87EC` the same way. For the race map's static references the record shows exactly the number of distinct absolute/long instructions the map counts: `$00:0300` 9 of 9, `$00:0302` 7 of 7, `$00:0313` 2 of 2 (window 1500–2100; `$00:4302` 35 of 39 in the window, the rest execute outside it).

### Part B — the fields

6. **Discovery by differencing** (attempts 3–8 of the task record, predictions written before each run). Every variant validated in two identical fresh runs. Against the baseline: `right-released-2000` diverges at 2000 in six bytes (`$0313`, `$0319`, `$0401`, `$04BB`, `$0BEB`, `$0BEC`); `left-instead` at 1500 in `$0313` (`0x02`); `down-instead-of-up` at 2200 in `$0313` (`0x05` vs `0x09`) and `$0315` (`0x02` vs `0x00`) with identical final states; `b-held` at 1500 in `$0313` (`0x81`); `y-held` at 1500 in 39 bytes including `$0313` (`0x41`); `a-held`, `x-held` in `$0311` (`0x80`, `0x40`); `l-held`, `r-held` in `$0311` (`0x20`, `0x10`) plus `$0329`/`$032D`. Per-frame series of `$0000–$21FF` from every run (26 MiB each) gave the time series; the Right-removed run separates the player's instance (`$0415` frozen at 1088) from the opponent's (`$0417` moving identically to the baseline, 1088 → 22234).
7. **Position and displacement.** `pos_x` (`$0415`, u16) is constant at 1088 through frame 1533 and rises monotonically to 22223 at 2999; for all 565 frames 1535–2100 in the access record, `pos[f+1] − pos[f]` equals the signed word stored to `$0BBB` in frame f+1 (values 14, 15, 16); over the whole series the same relation holds in 1459 of 1459 frames 1540–2999. With Left instead of Right the position falls to 990 by 1540 and reaches only 1133 at 2999.
8. **Speed, throttle and timer.** `speed` (`$04BB`, s16) is 456 at the GO and 481 at 2000; after the release it decreases by exactly 1 per frame for 100 frames (2000–2100, checked frame by frame); with Right held it also falls on the climb at 1600–2100 (481 → 448) while `throttle` (`$0BEB`, s16) stays at 432. The throttle ramps +16 per frame from 1503 to 432 at 1530 and is 0 from the first frame after release; with Left it ramps −16 per frame to −528. The timer's frame counter `$0E29` first increments at frame 1535 and cycles 0–4 (tenths `$0E25` at 1539, 1544, …; seconds `$0E21` at 1584; tens `$0E1D` at 2034): five frames per tenth from origin frame 1534, i.e. 50 updates per second, and the frame images read `0:01:3` at 1600 (65 frames after 1535) and `0:17:3` at 2400 (865 frames), as the digits predict.
9. **Writers and cadence.** Over 1500–2100 each field's primary writer stores exactly once in every frame: `$80:87EC`/`$80:87F2` (joypad images), `$82:AB36`/`$82:AB52` (axes), `$82:8DB9` and `$82:92A7` (player/opponent position, also over 1329–1533: 205 of 205 frames), `$82:8DF8` (displacement), `$82:8DBF` (throttle), `$82:8E9A` (speed, 1671 stores over 1329–2999), `$81:AE87` (camera); the timer frames counter is written by `$81:C6C9` in 566 frames and reset by `$81:C6ED` in the 113 frames whose tenth advanced. One game update is therefore one PAL frame in this scenario, from frame 1329 (the first frame after the NMI-off load, R-0006 finding 11) on, with the race itself starting at 1534. During the loading phase 1208–1328 the record holds 1,945,202 instructions and 829,311 accesses (15,941 unresolved).
10. **In-frame order and capture phase** (frame 1600, sequence numbers of the record): joypad reads and image stores at 566–568 (`$80:87EC`, `$80:87F2`), input interpretation at 801–840 (`$82:AAA4` reads `$0311`, `$82:AADF` reads `$0313`, axes written), the player's physics at 1153–2211 (`$82:8AB1` reads `pos_x`, `$82:8ABC` the throttle, `$82:8AF5` the displacement; `$82:8DB9` writes `pos_x`, `$82:8DBF` the throttle, `$82:8DF8` the displacement), the opponent's at 2361–3245, camera at 3722, jump value at 3698–4333, timer at 4475–4477, the copies at 6034–7410, the sample point after the frame's 18,000-odd instructions. The sample at the end of frame N therefore reflects the inputs presented for frame N: the joypad image changes in the frame the button is applied (1500, 2000, 2200 in the variants) and the physics of the same frame consume it (the throttle reacts at 1503 only because `$83:E794` overrides `axis_h` to neutral during 1500–1502).
11. **B, Y, lean.** B: from frame 1534 `$0331` = 1 and `$033D` (u16, 1079 at rest) rises to 1111 and 1145 while `pos_x` equals the baseline's in every one of the 3000 frames (a jump that does not change the track position). Y: the rider never leaves the start (`pos_x` 1088, `speed` 0 throughout, throttle saturated); mechanism not interpreted. Up vs Down: exactly two bytes of the whole 128 KiB work RAM differ at the end of frame 2230 (`$0313`, `$0315`; whole-WRAM dumps from `--stop-after-frame 2230`) and the final states are identical, so no persistent lean state exists in this scenario.
12. **Fields manifest.** `race-crawler-dragster-3000-fields` (the eleven fields declared as `wram_range` fields) validates in two identical fresh runs (exit 0, digests written), and against `race-crawler-dragster-3000-right-removed-fields` the first divergence at frame 1500 names `joy1h_image` (`0x01` vs `0x00`), localized to that one byte.

### Tooling checks (ROM-free)

13. `tests/tooling/test_access.py` (25 tests): every opcode decodes under every M/X/E combination; the kind of every data-operand mnemonic; hand-derived effective addresses for direct page (native, 16-bit D wrap, emulation page wraps for `dp,X` and the `(dp,X)` pointer, no wrap for `[dp]`), absolute and long (bank crossing), stack relative, every indirect form's deferral and resolution, indirect jumps taking the pointer value from the next pc, pushes (register, width, S decrement, page-1 wrap for the 6502-era forms only) and pulls, block moves in both directions and index widths; the key encoding; the streaming derivation on a synthetic ROM with a stub ring carrying full registers (values, counts, first/last frames, ROM read ranges and bitmap, the four resolution labels including work RAM code, the residual, the DMA log, watch logs, byte-identical documents, validation, the query's byte-span and mirror matching, the series writer, the resolver rules); the worker's argument checks and the CLI exit codes 2 and 3. `test --suite synthetic` grows from 148 to 173 checks.

## Interpretation

The record answers M1-02's question without touching the core: for every field the instruction that stores it, the value stored, the order within the frame and the once-per-frame cadence are read off the same trace M1-01 captured, and the residual is small and located (pointer chains advanced by `INC`). The four fields the task asked to validate with pre-registered predictions behave as predicted — position monotonic and equal to the accumulated displacement, speed decaying by a constant after release, timer at five PAL frames per tenth matching the screen, the joypad images bit-exact — while the two it allowed to remain unknown (lean, a trick) are recorded with the experiments that failed to find a state: Up/Down leaves no trace beyond the derived axis byte, and B changes a height-like value without changing the track position. The prediction that missed is instructive: the `$4218` image sits at `$0311`, not `$0312` — the record, not the guess, fixed the address.

What the interpretation does not cover: the arithmetic inside the physics routine (`$82:8AB1`–`$82:8DF8`) that turns throttle, speed and track direction into the displacement (the record gives its reads and writes, not its instructions), whether the speed decay of 1 per frame is friction or the slope at that track section, and every other track and rider. What would falsify the claims: a fresh capture with a different digest, a riding frame in which `pos[f+1] − pos[f] ≠ displacement[f+1]`, a frame in which a primary writer stores twice or not at all, or a timer digit that does not advance every fifth frame.
